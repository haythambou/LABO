⌨️ HARDWARE KEYBOARD MAPPINGS

🎛️ DECK A (LEFT)

  • Space Play / Pause
  • C Cue Point (Set or Hold)
  • S Beat Sync
  • 1 - 4 Performance Pads 1-4
  • Q, W, E, R Performance Pads 5-8
  • Shift + Q Pitch Bend -
  • Shift + W Pitch Bend +

🎛️ DECK B (RIGHT)

  • Enter Play / Pause
  • K Cue Point (Set or Hold)
  • L Beat Sync
  • 7 - 0 Performance Pads 1-4
  • U, I, O, P Performance Pads 5-8
  • Shift + U Pitch Bend -
  • Shift + I Pitch Bend +

🎚️ MIXER & GENERAL

  • ← / → Nudge Crossfader Left / Right
  • Down Arrow Reset Crossfader to Center (50/50 mix)
  • A, D Headphone Source Select (Cue A / Cue B / Master)
  • Drag Knobs Click & Drag mouse vertically on EQs/Filters to adjust!
